1. Project Overview & Epics Breakdown

The Chronic Disease Health Risk Assessment and Patient Monitoring System was engineered to solve the critical clinical bottleneck of delayed identification of Heart Disease, Diabetes, and Stroke. The project is organized into 8 core Epics and 14 Stories:

Epic 1: Data Collection & Extraction: Synthesized and extracted 6,000 realistic clinical records into raw CSV and SQLite EMR databases.
Epic 2: Data Preparation: Standardized WHO BMI, AHA BP stages, ADA glucose tiers, and multi-morbidity calculations.
Epic 3: Data Visualizations: Built 15 unique visualizations across clinical, behavioral, and demographic domains.
Epic 4: Responsive Dashboards: Designed 3 role-tailored dashboards for Dr. Carter, Emily Rodriguez, and Dr. Priya Nair.
Epic 5: Tableau Story: Crafted a 5-scene clinical narrative walking from cohort baseline to proactive intervention.
Epic 6: Performance Testing: Verified latency under 20ms on 6,000 records across 25 calculated fields.
Epic 7: Web Integration: Deployed a Flask/Python web interface with REST endpoints and an interactive AI Risk Agent.
Epic 8: Documentation & Demo: Compiled comprehensive guides, video narration scripts, and architectural blueprints.

2. Step-by-Step Project Development Procedure

Step 1: Clinical Domain Modeling & Synthetic Data Generation

Engineered scripts/generate_dataset.py to model multi-factorial disease etiologies. Framingham risk scores, American Diabetes Association fasting plasma guidelines, and American Heart Association hypertension criteria were integrated to simulate correlated physiological interactions. Output saved to data/raw/chronic_disease_raw.csv and data/raw/hospital_emr_database.db.

Step 2: Feature Engineering & Data Pipeline

Constructed scripts/data_preparation.py to bin continuous variables into discrete clinical cohorts (Age Groups, BMI Categories, Blood Pressure Stages). Multi-morbidity count and lifestyle risk indexes were formulated. Processed records were committed to data/processed/chronic_disease_analytics.db with B-Tree indexes on triage tiers.

Step 3: Tableau Workbook XML Construction (.twb)

Authored scripts/build_tableau_workbook.py to programmatically build tableau/Chronic_Disease_Risk_Analysis.twb with complete XML schemas, datasource connections to tableau_dataset.csv, 25 calculated fields, 15 worksheets, 3 dashboards, and 1 story point collection.

Step 4: Flask Web Application Architecture

Developed app/app.py with modular routing for each stakeholder scenario, RESTful JSON APIs for patient data retrieval, and an integrated Clinical AI Risk Assessment Agent engine in app/risk_engine.py.

Step 5: Testing, Benchmarking & Repository Publishing

Conducted performance load tests, filter response audits, and cross-browser responsiveness validation. Packaged the entire platform with Git version control, ready for direct GitHub hosting.
